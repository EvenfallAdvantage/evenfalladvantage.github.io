Overwatch Data Sources Notice

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Third-party data is provided as-is and may be delayed, inaccurate or unavailable. Overwatch is not for emergency, life-safety or navigation use. In an emergency, call 911.

Overwatch displays data from the providers listed below. Each provider is credited as its licence requires, and the credits are also shown on the map while the relevant layer is visible. This Notice forms part of our Terms of Service (Section 16). Your use of each provider's data is also subject to that provider's licence or terms. No provider endorses Evenfall Advantage LLC or Overwatch, and the inclusion of government data does not imply government approval.

Overwatch does not record, archive or rehost traffic-camera images. Images are loaded directly from the operating agency when you view them. See our Privacy Policy.

Live ships (AIS)

The Operations map's Air & Sea > Ships layer shows vessel positions from the Automatic Identification System (AIS). Positions come from aisstream.io (global, community receivers) and from Fintraffic's Digitraffic open data (Finland and the Baltic), licensed under CC BY 4.0: “Source: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi”. Overwatch keeps only each vessel's latest position for 30 minutes. AIS is self-reported by ships, can be minutes old, wrong or switched off, and must not be used for navigation or safety-of-life decisions.
