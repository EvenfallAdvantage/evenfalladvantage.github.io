Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Evenfall Advantage LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our event security guard training platform and services.

By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register for an account on our platform
  • Enroll in training courses or certification programs
  • Contact us for support or inquiries
  • Subscribe to our newsletters or communications
  • Participate in surveys or evaluations

This information may include:

  • Full name and contact information (email address, phone number, mailing address)
  • Date of birth and government-issued identification numbers (as required for certification)
  • Educational background and work experience
  • Training progress, assessment scores, and certification records
  • Payment and billing information
  • Emergency contact information
  • State-specific licensing or registration information

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access our platform, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages viewed, time spent on pages, navigation patterns)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Login timestamps and session information

2.3 Training and Assessment Data

We collect and maintain records of:

  • Course completion status and progress
  • Assessment and examination results
  • Certificate issuance and expiration dates
  • Attendance records for scheduled classes
  • Interactive scenario responses and performance metrics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our training platform and educational services
  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account, authenticate your identity, and provide customer support
  • Certification: To issue, track, and verify security guard certifications and credentials
  • Communication: To send you course updates, notifications, certificates, and administrative messages
  • Compliance: To comply with legal requirements, including state-specific security guard training mandates
  • Quality Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, enhance course content, and improve user experience
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or security threats
  • Marketing: To send promotional materials, newsletters, and updates about new courses (with your consent)

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We May Share Your Information With:

  • Regulatory Authorities: State agencies, licensing boards, and law enforcement as required by law or for certification verification
  • Employers and Hiring Agencies: With your consent, we may verify your training completion and certification status to prospective or current employers
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist us in operating our platform, processing payments, or delivering services
  • Educational Partners: Accredited institutions or certification bodies involved in course delivery or credential issuance

4.2 We Do Not:

  • Sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes
  • Share your information with unaffiliated third parties without your consent, except as described in this policy

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security practices include:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest using SSL/TLS protocols
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure data backup and disaster recovery procedures

Important: While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify you promptly of any data breach affecting your information as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Specifically:

  • Training Records: Maintained for at least 7 years as required by industry regulations and certification standards
  • Certification Records: Retained indefinitely for verification and credential validation purposes
  • Account Information: Retained for the duration of your account activity, plus applicable retention periods
  • Payment Information: Retained as required for tax, accounting, and legal compliance

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Portability: Request a copy of your information in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 11. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our platform. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us:

  • Remember your login credentials and preferences
  • Track your progress through training modules
  • Analyze platform usage and performance
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Note that disabling cookies may limit certain functionality of our platform.

8A. Overwatch Map Data and Third-Party Providers

The Overwatch Operations map shows third-party data such as public traffic-camera images, aircraft transponder broadcasts, satellite and weather imagery, and basemap tiles (see our Data Sources Notice). This data may incidentally show or relate to individuals or vehicles. Evenfall does not record or archive third-party camera images. Images are loaded directly from the operating agency each time you view them. We store only camera locations, names, image addresses and similar metadata.

When your browser loads a third-party image, map tile or feed, that provider receives your IP address, browser information and the request under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. Some data is fetched by our servers instead, in which case the provider sees our server rather than you. Overwatch stores a small number of preferences in your browser's local storage, such as your map layer settings and whether you have acknowledged the traffic-camera notice.

Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

This section applies to California residents and supplements the rest of this policy. Where we process personal information on behalf of a security company that uses Overwatch (for example, a guard's schedule, timesheets or patrol logs), we act as that company's service provider, and requests about that information may be passed to the company that controls it.

13.1 Categories of personal information we collect

In the last 12 months we collected the following categories, from you, your employer, or your devices, for the purposes in Section 3:

  • Identifiers: name, email address, phone number, account ID, IP address.
  • Professional or employment information: role, company, schedules, timesheets, guard card and license numbers and expiry dates, training and certification records, work history.
  • Geolocation data: precise device location when you clock in or out, scan patrol checkpoints, send an SOS alert, or share your location with your team.
  • Audio and electronic information: photos, videos and signatures you attach to reports. Dictation audio is transcribed on your device and is not sent to us; only the text you save is stored.
  • Internet or network activity: sign-in events, security logs and error logs.
  • Commercial information: invoices and billing records, where applicable.
  • Sensitive personal information: precise geolocation, government-issued license or ID numbers, and account log-in credentials.

We disclose these categories only to the service providers listed in our subprocessor list (hosting, database, email, push notifications and SMS) and to your employer, for the business purposes described in this policy.

13.2 Sensitive personal information

We use sensitive personal information only to provide the services you or your employer requested (for example, verifying a clock-in location, routing an SOS alert, or checking that a guard card is current), to keep accounts secure, and as required by law. We do not use it to infer characteristics about you. Because we only use it for these permitted purposes, we do not offer a separate "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link. Location is collected only while you use those features; you can turn off location in your device settings, though some features (such as verified clock-in) will not work without it.

13.3 We do not sell or share personal information

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the last 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

13.4 Global Privacy Control

Our websites honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal. If your browser sends GPC, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of non-essential cookies, and we do not show you the cookie banner.

13.5 Your rights

  • Right to know / access: the categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold about you. Signed-in users can download a copy at any time from Security → Privacy → Export my data.
  • Right to delete: signed-in users can use Security → Privacy → Erase my data. Some records (for example, timesheets and incident reports your employer must keep) are retained in anonymized form where the law or a legitimate business purpose requires it.
  • Right to correct: you can edit most profile information in Profile. For anything you can't edit, ask us or your company admin.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: we do not sell or share, so there is nothing to opt out of; GPC is honored regardless.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: see Section 13.2.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not deny service, charge different prices or provide a different quality of service because you exercised these rights.

13.6 How to make a request

Email privacy@evenfalladvantage.com or use the in-app tools above. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days (we may extend once by another 45 days and will tell you why). To protect you, we verify your identity by matching information we already hold, usually by confirming control of your account email. You may use an authorized agent; we will ask for your signed permission and may ask you to verify your identity directly.

13.7 Retention

We keep each category only as long as needed for the purposes above: account and profile data for as long as the account is active; timesheets, incident reports and related media for as long as your employer's records policy and applicable law require (typically up to 7 years); security, audit and error logs for up to 1 year; and deleted-account data is removed or anonymized within 30 days of the request, except where retention is legally required. Backups roll off within 7 days.
