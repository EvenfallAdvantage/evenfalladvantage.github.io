    Overview

    Why Overwatch Tactical

    Security teams work at events, remote sites and places where coverage drops out. Overwatch Tactical puts the whole shift in one app (who's on, where they are, what's happening and what's been reported) and keeps it usable without a connection.

    Online, it's the everyday Overwatch platform. Offline, it runs on ODIN, a portable node with its own Wi-Fi and database. Chat falls back from Wi-Fi to the BIFROST LoRa mesh to a local cache, and the map shows when positions are live, aging or stale.

    What it does

    Features

    • Clock and badge scan

      Clock in and out, scan badges at posts, and request leave. Bulk Mass Clock is on the way.

    • Operations map

      Live team positions with tactical symbols, drawing, measuring, range rings and routes, plus CoT import/export for ATAK.

    • Patrols

      Plan and run patrol tours with checkpoint scans.

    • Incidents and field reports

      Log incidents and file field reports and forms from the phone in your hand.

    • Briefings and dashboards

      Pinned briefings first, then a dashboard tuned to each role, from officer to command.

    • Comms and Radio Scanner

      Team channels, BIFROST LoRa messaging and a Radio Scanner tab with SDR transcripts.

    • Personnel and approvals

      Roster, forms, applicants, onboarding and timesheet approvals in one place.

    • Academy

      Courses, de-escalation scenarios, certifications, a field manual and state-law reference.

    • Invoices and ops planning

      Invoicing and event planning for the people running the operation.

    • HQ Config

      Admins choose which features each company sees, so the app shows only what a team uses.

    Operations map

    TAK's power, without the clutter

    One floating toolbar covers Select, Marker, Draw, Measure, Rings and Route. Symbols are picked by plain-language search ("medic", "threat", "LZ") with recents, not raw codes. Undo and redo are always one tap away, layers are simple toggles, and CoT import/export keeps it working alongside ATAK.

    Runs on ODIN

    Fully offline in the field

    On an ODIN node, Overwatch Tactical runs with a local database and offline map tiles, served over the node's own ODINet5G hotspot. Team chat bridges onto the BIFROST LoRa mesh, so radios beyond Wi-Fi still get messages, and the Radio Scanner tab shows transcripts from the node's SDR scanner.
