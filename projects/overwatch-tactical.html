Overwatch Tactical Edition

Your whole operation, in a case. No internet required.

A self-contained, airgapped build of the Overwatch workforce platform. It runs on a portable field server, creates its own WiFi network and reaches personnel beyond WiFi over an encrypted LoRa mesh.

See how it worksTalk to us

Why Overwatch Tactical Edition

Security, emergency-response and remote-site teams often work where the internet is unavailable, unreliable or not allowed. Cloud software can't follow them there. Overwatch Tactical Edition brings the platform with them: one rugged case that powers on, broadcasts its own network and keeps everyone connected. Nothing leaves the box.

Capabilities

  • Zero internet dependency

    Every service runs on the box. No cloud calls or outside telemetry.

  • Field-portable

    Rugged-case compute with a separate power unit. Up to about 35 hours on battery (estimate, Config A).

  • Mesh comms

    Text and GPS positions over LoRa radios, about 1–5 km on the ground, further with relays.

  • Hardened by design

    Encrypted storage, a private certificate authority and signed updates.

  • Scales with the team

    Three configurations, from about 10 users to 200+.

  • No SaaS fees

    No cloud subscriptions to keep it running.

What's in the kit

Tap a component to learn what it does.

Simplified: radios → LoRa gateway → server ← WiFi ← phones.

  1. Field server. Runs the full Overwatch platform locally: app, database, maps and messaging.
  2. WiFi access point. Broadcasts a private, encrypted network for phones and laptops nearby.
  3. LoRa gateway. Bridges the long-range mesh into the platform, so radio users appear on the map.
  4. Power unit. A separate battery case. It runs the kit for about a day and a half (estimate), and solar can extend it.
