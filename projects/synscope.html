Design estimates and targets

    Overview

    Why SynScope

    Situational awareness usually means juggling separate devices. SynScope puts several sensors in one sealed pod on a standard rail. Two mmWave radars and a thermal camera look ahead, while GPS, a compass and a motion sensor add position and heading. The results appear on a 1.28-inch round display, angled so it can be read without breaking position.

    What it does

    Capabilities

    • Sensor fusion

      Two mmWave radars plus a thermal camera, combined into one simple view.

    • Knows where it points

      GPS, compass and IMU give every reading a position and bearing.

    • Glanceable display

      A 1.28" round screen with a large, high-contrast layout.

    • Built for the canted mount

      Designed to sit on a 45° offset rail mount, clear of the primary optic.

    • Sealed by design

      No vents or light pipes. Heat leaves through the walls. IP54 is the design target.

    • Glove-friendly control

      One rotary knob with push to select, usable with gloves.

    • Self-levelling display

      The motion sensor tracks how far the pod is tilted, and the picture turns to stay level with the ground. If that sensor is unavailable, the display falls back to a fixed correction for the 45° mount. In development

    Picatinny rail, designed for a 45° offset mount
    Controls
    Single rotary knob with push-select
    Display levelling
    Self-levelling to the horizon using the IMU; fixed 45° fallback (in development)
    Power
    Internal rechargeable battery, USB-C charging
    Ingress
    IP54 design target (not yet tested)

    In design, prototype build underway
